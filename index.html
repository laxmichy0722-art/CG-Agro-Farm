About Us

About CG-Agro-Farm

CG-Agro-Farm is a diversified business organization combining agriculture, food processing, livestock, furniture manufacturing and aquaculture.

Instead of running four separate businesses, the company manages every division through one central management system — covering sales, purchases, expenses, inventory, production, staff and daily operations. This keeps quality consistent, costs visible and customers informed across all four divisions.

Division 01

CG Rice Mill

A complete paddy-to-rice operation — from intake and cleaning to milling, grading, packaging, storage and distribution.

Rice Processing

Controlled milling from raw paddy to finished rice.

Cleaning

Removal of dust, stones and foreign material before milling.

Milling

Modern milling machinery for consistent grain quality.

Grading

Sorting by grain size, colour and broken percentage.

Packaging

Clean, clearly labelled packs in practical sizes.

Storage

Dry, ventilated storage to protect quality after packing.

Distribution

Wholesale supply and local delivery to retailers.

Rice Mill Process

  1. 1

    Raw Paddy

    Incoming stock recorded and weighed.

  2. 2

    Cleaning

    Separators and destoners clean the grain.

  3. 3

    Dehusking

    Husks and bran are separated from the grain.

  4. 4

    Milling

    Grain polished to the required grade.

  5. 5

    Grading

    Rice sorted into quality categories.

  6. 6

    Packaging

    Weighed, sealed and labelled.

  7. 7

    Distribution

    Dispatched to wholesale and local buyers.

Quality Assurance

Checked at every stage

Quality is verified at intake, after cleaning, after milling and before dispatch, so customers receive rice that matches the agreed grade every time.

View Rice Mill SOP
  • Moisture & cleanliness check at intake
  • Foreign material removal
  • Broken grain percentage recorded
  • Pack weight verified before sealing
  • Batch numbers written for traceability
